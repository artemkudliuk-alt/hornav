#03 • Global Framework

International Maritime Organization

The Company monitors and applies relevant IMO instruments, codes and amendments applicable to its vessels and operations, including requirements relating to safety, environmental protection, vessel operation and certification.

#04 • Vessel Safety

SOLAS Convention

The Company verifies that vessels meet applicable safety requirements for construction, equipment and operation, including required safety equipment, certification and operational standards compatible with safety of life at sea.

SOLAS 1974 Convention ↗
#05 • Decarbonization & Environment

MARPOL & Decarbonization

Minimizing ship-generated pollution (oil, garbage, sewage, air emissions). Reducing carbon footprint through active management of vessel efficiency indicators, including CII, EEXI, waste management, and ballast water treatment systems.

MARPOL Convention (CII & EEXI) ↗
#06 • Seafarer Competency

STCW Convention

The Company verifies that seafarers hold the required qualifications, certificates, training and competencies for their positions and that applicable watchkeeping requirements are strictly observed across all bridge and engine operations.

STCW Human Element Standards ↗
#07 • Quality Policy Age > 20 Yrs Audit

Vessel Quality & Age Standards

Selective approach to vessel employment considering age, class status, technical condition, safety track record, and healthcare. Vessels over 20 years of age are considered only following satisfactory technical evaluation and confirmation of compliance.

Danamira Internal Technical Assessment Standard
#08 • Seafarer Healthcare Protocol 5-Stage Verification

Crew Medical Fitness & Pre-Employment Screening

Every seafarer proposed for employment is subject to rigorous medical review before final approval and deployment. Particular attention is given to cardiovascular conditions, surgery histories, diabetes, long-term medication, and previous medical repatriation.

(1) Valid Medical Fitness-for-Duty Certificate
(2) Signed Health Declaration / Medical History
(3) Hospital discharge & clinical summaries
(4) Specialist medical reports & diagnostic tests
(5) Valid Seafarer Employment Agreement (SEA) & documentation

#09 • Labor Rights

MLC, 2006 (ILO)

Applying international standards concerning Seafarer Employment Agreements, working and rest hours, wages, repatriation, medical care onboard and ashore, accommodation, food, and seafarer welfare.

Maritime Labour Convention 2006 ↗
#10 • Flag State Audits

Flag State Implementation

For each vessel, the Company verifies compliance with requirements of the vessel's Flag Administration, including applicable statutory certification, annual surveys, documentation and enforcement of IMO conventions.

IMO Flag State Implementation ↗
#11 • Class Certification

Classification Society (IACS)

Every vessel managed or operated is required to maintain valid Class status with an IACS member classification society, with continuous survey compliance and technical certification verified vessel-by-vessel.

iacs.org.uk Portal ↗
#12 • Commercial Clauses

Commercial & Technical Due Diligence

The Company conducts commercial and technical due diligence in accordance with applicable BIMCO standards and clauses, together with established maritime industry practices. Prior to fixture, each vessel and cargo is assessed for suitability, valid class and statutory status, technical condition, safety, insurance, cargo requirements and operational risks. Each vessel must be classed with an IACS member classification society. Fixtures are concluded only following satisfactory completion of due diligence and agreement of the applicable contractual terms.

Bulk Shipping Quality Clause (2000) ↗ Dry Bulk Marine Risk Assessment Clause (2013) ↗
#13 • Fleet Operations 24/7 Operations

Fleet & Cargo Operations

The Company optimizes fleet performance, ensuring the safe and efficient transportation and distribution of cargoes to various ports in accordance with the terms and conditions of each transportation contract.

Danamira Fleet Operations & Route Optimization Protocol
#14 • Sanctions Screening

Sanctions Clause & Screening

The Company conducts sanctions and counterparty screening before entering into any fixture or contractual commitment. Relevant vessels, owners, charterers, counterparties, cargoes, ports and trading parties are checked against applicable international sanctions requirements.

The Company applies applicable BIMCO sanctions clauses in its contractual arrangements and does not proceed with transactions where sanctions restrictions, prohibited activities or unacceptable compliance risks are identified.
