About Danamira Shipping

Mission, Vision
& Corporate Values

We are committed to safe, efficient and reliable seaborne transportation through professional ship management, built on integrity, expertise and long-term partnerships.

Mission

Efficient seaborne transportation of dry-bulk, non-dangerous, non-inflammable, non-IMO cargoes through the commercial and technical operation of Bulk Carriers and General Cargo vessels – ensuring reliability, compliance, operational performance across international trades.

Vision

Long-term development through strategic fleet expansion and continuous growth of the Company's maritime operations. Future plans include the consideration of newbuilding projects, particularly in relation to new vessel framework, design, and construction development.

Values

Compliance Total transparency and strict compliance with regulations.
Operations Optimizing fleet performance for safe and efficient operations.
Standards Operating under the Greek maritime framework.
Reliability Dependable execution in every commitment.
